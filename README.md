# ClaimBack India

Build a mobile-first web app "ClaimBack" that helps people in India appeal rejected or reduced health insurance claims.

Routes: / (landing), /login, /new-claim, /claim/:id (results), /claim/:id/letter, /privacy, /terms, /admin.

Landing page: hero "Claim rejected? Find out if you can fight it, in 5 minutes", short "how it works" in 4 steps (upload, analysis, appeal letter, escalation steps), common rejection reasons (pre-existing disease, room rent deduction, non-payable items, waiting period, missing documents), trust section on privacy, FAQ, clear disclaimer that this is drafting help and not legal advice.

Design: calm, reassuring, teal and white, generous spacing, large readable text, simple language. Email + OTP or magic-link auth.
